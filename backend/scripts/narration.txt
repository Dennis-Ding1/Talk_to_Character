# --- Intro ---
<|emotion:enthusiasm|>Have you ever wanted to pause a movie and ask a character what they're really thinking?
That's what we built. Pause any scene, click on a face, and the character talks back, in their own voice, with a face that moves. It all runs on Boson AI's Higgs models.

# --- Up to now ---
In Up to now mode, the character only knows what has happened so far in the film, so nothing gets spoiled.

# --- Omniscience ---
In Omniscience mode, the character knows their whole story, so you can ask them what if.

# --- Voice ---
In Voice mode, you just talk. The character answers in about a second, and you can cut in at any time.

# --- Avatar ---
In Avatar mode, the reply becomes a talking face, based on how the character looks in that very scene.
And also, you can ask or listen in different languages.
