Emotion has a price, too. Realtime audio is the fastest path, but its emotion comes only from how the persona is written. TTS 3 takes explicit emotion and prosody tags, and that costs one and a half to two seconds more. So each film chooses.

Limits

1

Lip-sync takes time, and emotion takes more

The face starts 3–6 s after the reply is written, and TTS 3 emotion tags add 1.5–2.3 s. Realtime audio is the fast fallback, but it can’t take emotion tags.

2

Animated faces need hand setup

Face detection is trained on real people. Each animated character needs its own profile, and its avatar looks less convincing.

3

The guard is itself an LLM

The time filter is tested, but how often the guard misses a spoiler or blocks a harmless line hasn’t been measured.

4

Every new film takes setup

Subtitles, ideally a screenplay, LLM summaries that can be wrong, and some hand fixes to who said what.

What's still hard. Lip-sync takes seconds, and longer with emotion tags. Animated faces need hand setup, because face models are trained on real people. The guard is an LLM too, and we haven't measured how often it misses. And every new film takes setup.
